I chose this song because I go in and out of massive Tom Waits phases about once a year and this project happened to be in the middle of one. I wanted to reflect the antiquated and dingy vibe all of his songs have so I chose black and white with the typewriter font to set a noir mood. I wanted it to be puzzle-ish almost while still reflecting the surreal nature of the song so I thought I'd find equally surreal ways to depict the content of the lyrics.
